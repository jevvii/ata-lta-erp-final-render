# 1. Welcome to Your ERP System (The Big Picture)

## 1.1 What is this system and why do we use it?
Welcome to the **ATA & LTA ERP Platform**! Think of this system as our accounting firm's **central digital office**. 

In the past, accounting and legal compliance work required keeping track of dozens of Excel spreadsheets, chasing coworkers on chat apps for expense receipts, searching physical file cabinets for client certificates, and manually calculating taxes. 

This ERP system brings everything together into one easy-to-use web screen:
* **Client Files:** Every client's legal name, Tax ID (TIN), business registration, and contact persons are stored in one master directory.
* **Work Requests & Task Lists:** Every client engagement (like annual tax filings, business permits, or SEC updates) is tracked step-by-step from start to finish.
* **Billing & Official Receipts:** Invoices are created automatically with 12% VAT and BIR Form 2307 deductions, and payments are tracked so no client bills are forgotten.
* **Office Expenses & Cash Advances:** Whenever you need money to pay government filing fees at the BIR or SEC, you can request and receive approved cash vouchers right here.
* **Document Vault (DMS):** You always know exactly which cabinet or shelf holds original client certificates, and you can view scanned PDF copies in seconds.
* **Messenger & Courier Tracking:** Every document delivered to a client via Grab Express or LBC has a signed delivery receipt attached so papers never get lost.

---

## 1.2 Two Companies, One Simple Screen (ATA & LTA)
Our practice operates under two sister companies:
1. **Alliance Tax & Accounting (ATA):** Specializing in bookkeeping, financial statements, and corporate tax compliance.
2. **Ledger Tax & Accounting (LTA):** Specializing in business registrations, government permits, SEC compliance, and corporate legal advisory.

**You do NOT need two separate usernames or passwords!** If you work for both companies, you log in once. At the top of your screen, there is a company selector:
* Select **ATA** to work on ATA client accounts.
* Select **LTA** to work on LTA client accounts.
* If you are a General Manager or Partner, you can choose **ALL** to view a combined bird's-eye summary of both firms on one screen.

---

## 1.3 How to Read Your Screen: The 4 View Modes
Whenever you open a client profile, work request, or invoice, the system allows you to choose how the information displays so you can work comfortably:
1. **Side Peek (Slide-Out):** The form slides out smoothly from the right side of your screen. This is perfect when you want to look at a form while still reading the main list behind it.
2. **Center Peek (Pop-up Box):** The form appears centered on your screen like a focused window. Great for quick updates.
3. **Full Page:** The form expands across your entire monitor. Ideal when entering long invoices with many line items or reading lengthy document checklists.
4. **New Tab:** Opens the record in a fresh browser tab. Great if you use two monitors and want to compare two projects side-by-side.

> [!TIP]
> Click the small view-mode icon in the top-right corner of any form to pick your favorite layout. You can check *"Save as default"* and the system will remember your choice every time you log in!

### 1. System Administrator (`Admin`)
* **Who they are:** Managing Partner, IT Director, or System Controller.
* **What they do:** Can see and do everything. They set up employee accounts, choose which companies employees can access, configure pre-made task templates, and inspect security audit logs.

### 2. General Manager (`Manager`)
* **Who they are:** Engagement Director or Senior Account Manager.
* **What they do:** Oversees client projects. They create new Work Requests, assign tasks to staff, check completed work, and make sure government filing deadlines are met. They can request invoices from Accounting and approve staff tasks.

### 3. Senior Accountant (`Accounting`)
* **Who they are:** Accounting Manager or Financial Controller.
* **What they do:** Controls all firm finances. They are the only people who can officially create client sales invoices, calculate tax withholdings, record client payments, configure monthly retainer templates, and release cash disbursement vouchers.

### 4. Operations Specialist (`Operations`)
* **Who they are:** Tax Filing Agent, Field Liaison, or Compliance Specialist.
* **What they do:** The frontline engine! They visit the BIR, SEC, and City Halls, check off task checklists, upload photos of stamped government forms, log their travel hours, and request filing fee money from Accounting.

### 5. Documentation Officer (`Documentation`)
* **Who they are:** Archivist, Vault Custodian, or Logistics Coordinator.
* **What they do:** Takes care of physical binders and original client papers. When documents must be delivered to a client, they draft official transmittal letters, book couriers (Grab/LBC), and make sure signed delivery slips are uploaded.

### 6. Human Resources Specialist (`HR`)
* **Who they are:** People Operations Officer.
* **What they do:** Monitors employee workload so team members don't get overwhelmed with too many tasks due on the same day. They verify time logs and submit internal office supply requests.

## 2.2 "Your Clean Desk Policy": Why You Only See What's Assigned to You
Have you ever noticed that you only see certain projects on your screen? That is intentional! We call it the **"Clean Desk Policy"**:
* When an Operations Specialist logs in, they only see projects where they are assigned to a task or checklist item.
* Unrelated client legal disputes, private partner billings, or other teams' tax returns are hidden.
* **Why this helps you:** Your screen stays clean and organized, free of clutter, and sensitive client information remains strictly confidential.

---

## 2.3 The "Second Pair of Eyes" Rule (Four-Eyes Approval)
To prevent accidental mistakes and protect company funds, our ERP uses the **Four-Eyes Principle**:
* If a staff member creates a new project, asks for cash, or marks an invoice as paid, the system does not immediately finalize it.
* Instead, it places the request in the **Pending Approvals** room.
* A second person (a General Manager or Senior Accountant) reviews the request, checks the details, and clicks **Approve**.
* **Rule:** You can never approve your own request! A second pair of eyes is always required.

# 3. Step-by-Step Daily Routine for Each Role

## 3.1 Daily Routine: System Administrator (IT & Firm Management)
1. **Morning Security Check:** Log in and open the **Pending Approvals** tab (`#admin/pending-approvals`). Review any special requests waiting for executive sign-off.
2. **Team Account Maintenance:** If a new employee joins the firm, open `#admin/users`, click **"+ Add User"**, set their email, choose their role and department, and assign ATA, LTA, or both. If someone leaves, set their status to **Inactive** immediately.
3. **Master Task Template Tuning:** In `#operations`, click the **Task Templates** tab. Ensure all standard service procedures (like "Quarterly BIR 2551Q Filing") have up-to-date checklist items and estimated minutes.

---

## 3.2 Daily Routine: General Manager (Client Projects & Team Coordination)
1. **Morning Cockpit Review:** Open the **Dashboard** (`#dashboard`). Check the **Urgent Deadlines** card to see which tax returns or permits are due in the next 48 hours.
2. **Launch New Client Work:** Click **"+ New Work Request"** to start an engagement. Choose the client, name the project, pick the filing deadline, and submit it.
3. **Assign Work to Team Members:** In the project detail view, click **"+ Add Task"**. Choose a pre-made template (so the checklist fills out automatically), select the Operations Specialist responsible, and assign the deadline.
4. **Approve Completed Steps:** When staff complete tasks or request invoice issuance, review their submissions and click **Approve**.

---

## 3.3 Daily Routine: Senior Accountant (Client Invoices, Payments & Expenses)
1. **Check Incoming Requests:** Click the **Operations Requests** badge. See if any field staff have requested billing for completed work or need filing fee advances.
2. **Issue Client Invoices:** Open `#billing` and click **"+ New Invoice"**. Select the client, type the service description, and let the system calculate the 12% VAT and BIR Form 2307 Withholding Tax. Click **Issue Invoice**.
3. **Record Payments:** When clients deposit funds into our bank account, click **Record Payment** on the invoice, type the deposit reference number, and print the official receipt voucher.
4. **Release Expense Funds:** In `#disbursement`, review approved cash advance requests. Enter the check number or online bank transfer code and mark the voucher as **Funded**.

---

## 3.4 Daily Routine: Operations Specialist (Field Work, Permits & Checklists)
1. **Check Today's To-Do List:** Log in and look at your **End-of-Day (EOD) Reminder** on the Dashboard. Click on any task to jump straight to the project.
2. **Filter the Board by "My Tasks":** In `#operations`, click the **"My Tasks"** button so you only see your own active cards.
3. **Work Through the Checklist:** Open your task card and move it from `Todo` to `In Progress`. As you complete each step (e.g. "Gather Sales Book", "Compute Percentage Tax", "Visit BIR RDO"), check off the box.
4. **Attach Government Receiving Stamps:** Take a photo or scan of the official BIR receiving stamp, click **Upload Attachment**, and save it to the task.
5. **Log Your Hours:** Click **Log Time**, type the hours spent and a brief note (e.g., "2.5 hours - Queued at BIR RDO 044 for tax clearance").

---

## 3.5 Daily Routine: Documentation Officer (Vault Custody & Couriers)
1. **Cataloging Received Documents:** When a client drops off corporate papers, open `#dms`, click **"+ Add Document"**, record the physical binder code (e.g., `Cabinet A, Binder LTA-04`), scan the PDF, and save it.
2. **Tracking Borrowed Papers:** If an Operations Specialist takes original SEC papers to a government agency, click **Handover Document** and record who borrowed it and when it must be returned.
3. **Preparing Courier Deliveries:** Open `#transmittal`, click **"+ Create Transmittal"**, select the client, choose which papers are in the pouch, and pick the courier (Grab/LBC).
4. **Closing the Delivery Loop:** When the courier returns with the client's signature, click **Mark Acknowledged** and upload a photo of the signed receipt slip.

---

## 3.6 Daily Routine: Human Resources Specialist (Team Hours & Workload)
1. **Review Team Utilization:** Check the **Capacity Heatmap** on the Dashboard. If one Operations Specialist has 50 hours of work this week while another has only 15, let the General Manager know so work can be rebalanced.
2. **Audit Daily Time Logs:** Check `#reports` to ensure all staff members are logging their hours daily so client billing remains accurate.

### Action 6: Moving a Project through the 4 Progress Phases
* **Why you do this:** Every professional project follows four clear stages: Gathering information, doing the work, filing with the government, and delivering final receipts.
* **What you see on screen:** Inside the project detail view, you will see a progress bar with 4 stages: **Discovery**, **Execution**, **Filing**, and **Closeout**.

![Work Request Detail Showing 4 Phases](screenshots/09_work_request_detail_phases.png)

#### The 4 Phases Explained:
* 🔍 **1. Discovery:** Gathering client receipts, ledgers, and authorisations.
* ⚙️ **2. Execution:** Crunching the numbers, preparing tax returns, or drafting legal papers.
* 🏛️ **3. Filing:** Visiting the BIR, SEC, or City Hall to submit documents and secure stamps.
* 🏁 **4. Closeout:** Collecting payment, liquidating cash advances, delivering stamped originals by courier, and archiving the file.
* **How to advance:** When all tasks in a stage are completed, the General Manager clicks **Advance Phase** to unlock the next milestone!

# 5. Help! What Do I Do If Something Goes Wrong? (Troubleshooting Made Simple)

### Q: It says "Too Many Requests" when I try to log in. What happened?
* **Plain English Explanation:** If you type the wrong password several times in a row, the system temporarily locks the door for 15 minutes to prevent hackers from guessing passwords.
* **How to Fix:** Wait 15 minutes. Use the **Eye Icon** on the password box to verify your spelling before clicking Sign In.

---

### Q: My screen looks frozen, shows old numbers, or won't show a new tab. What should I do?
* **Plain English Explanation:** Your web browser sometimes saves an old picture of the website to load faster.
* **How to Fix (The Clean Refresh):**
  - On a Windows computer: Hold down `Ctrl` and `Shift`, then tap `R` (or press `Ctrl` + `F5`).
  - On a Mac computer: Hold down `Cmd` and `Shift`, then tap `R`.
  - The screen will reload with the latest code immediately!

---

### Q: Why can't I see a Work Request or Invoice that my coworker just created?
* **Plain English Explanation:** Remember our **Clean Desk Policy**! If you are not assigned to a task on that project, the system hides it to keep your screen tidy and confidential.
* **How to Fix:** Ask the project manager to add you as a worker or co-assignee on one of the tasks. The project will appear on your board instantly!

---

### Q: Why can't I click the 'ALL' company button?
* **Plain English Explanation:** The combined 'ALL' view is an executive view reserved for General Managers and Firm Partners who manage both ATA and LTA.
* **How to Fix:** If you are an operational staff member, simply choose **ATA** or **LTA** directly in the top dropdown.

---

### Q: My file upload failed with "File Too Large". What should I do?
* **Plain English Explanation:** Each uploaded document cannot exceed 25 Megabytes (MB).
* **How to Fix:** If you scanned a document at ultra-high resolution, compress the PDF using standard office software before uploading.

---

### Q: I clicked "Print Voucher" or "Download Invoice", but nothing popped up!
* **Plain English Explanation:** Your web browser thinks the PDF is an unwanted advertisement and blocked it.
* **How to Fix:** Look at the right side of your browser address bar. You will see a small icon with a red X. Click it and select *"Always allow pop-ups from this website"*.

# 6. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### 1. Can a client be shared between ATA and LTA?
**Yes!** Many clients retain ATA for monthly bookkeeping and LTA for legal compliance or Mayor's Permit renewals. When creating the client, simply check both **ATA** and **LTA** under Company Affiliation.

### 2. Can I delete a task if I made a mistake?
If a task is still in `Draft` or `Todo`, you can edit or delete it. However, if a task is already `Completed` and linked to an official payment or transmittal, it is locked to maintain audit records.

### 3. What is the difference between an Operations Request and a Disbursement?
An **Operations Request** is you asking for something (like an internal memo saying *"Please give me ₱5,000 for BIR filing fees"*). A **Disbursement** is the actual accounting voucher and check issued by Accounting.

### 4. What happens if I make a typo on an issued invoice?
Issued invoices represent legal tax claims. If an invoice has a mistake, do not delete it. Contact Senior Accounting; they will cancel the invoice with a formal explanation and issue a corrected replacement.

### 5. Where do I find the physical binder of a client's documents?
Open **DMS** (`#dms`), locate the document title, and look at the **Physical Location** column. It shows the exact cabinet, shelf, and binder number in our archive room.

### 6. Who approves the courier transmittal before dispatch?
To ensure no original client documents are sent to the wrong address, the **System Administrator** must click **Approve Dispatch** before couriers are called.
